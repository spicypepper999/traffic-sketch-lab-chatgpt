TRAFFIC SKETCH LAB / ALPHA 0.9

Build the network. Watch it move.

This is an interactive traffic engineering prototype. Every road is compiled into individual directional lanes and intersections before vehicles are simulated.

Build

Choose a road tool. Click once to start and again to finish. Enable Curved road to place a third point that bends the curve. New at-grade crossings compile into junctions automatically.

Set start/end elevations to build ramps. Roads crossing at different heights do not connect.

Inspect & control

Use Select to click a car and inspect its planned route, or drag road endpoints or curve handles. Use Lane links to select each inbound lane and toggle any outbound movement, including 1→2 splits and 2→1 merges. Use Trip nodes to assign sources and targets for several independent traffic pairs. Drag individual lane endpoint handles when an intersection is selected.

Spawn vehicles between your selected nodes, change the demand slider, and adjust car-following settings. Simulation runs in a separate Web Worker.
